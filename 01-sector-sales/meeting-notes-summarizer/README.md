# Meeting Notes Summarizer

**Version:** v1.0
**Sector:** Sales
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-28

## What This Skill Does

Transforms messy unstructured meeting notes into
clean professional summaries ready to send directly
to clients and senior management.

## Time Saved
- Before: 40-45 minutes per meeting
- After: Under 2 minutes
- Weekly saving (3 meetings): ~2 hours

## Quick Start

Invoke with:
/meeting-notes-summarizer

Required inputs:
- Meeting Date
- Participants with roles
- Meeting Topic
- Raw Meeting Notes

Optional inputs:
- Summary Style (default: Professional)
- Output Language (default: English)

## Output

A professionally formatted markdown document with:
- Executive Summary (3-5 sentences)
- Key Discussion Points
- Decisions Made
- Action Items (with owner, deadline, priority)
- Next Steps
- Next Meeting details
